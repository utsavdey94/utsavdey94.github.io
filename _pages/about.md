## Research & Technical Interests

- **Polymer Science & Functional Coatings** — polymer synthesis, emulsion polymerization, Janus particles, waterborne coatings, antimicrobial and omniphobic surfaces
- **Thin Films & Semiconductor Processing** — thin-film deposition, photolithography, oxidation, diffusion, wet and dry etching, plasma processing, wafer metrology
- **Nanomaterials & Surface Engineering** — colloidal synthesis, particle morphology control, surface modification, interfacial chemistry
- **Electrochemical & Energy Materials** — sodium- and lithium-ion battery materials, electrochemical characterization, solid-state materials
- **Process Development & Data Analysis** — DOE, SPC, statistical analysis, process optimization, scale-up, and structure–property correlations

## Selected Highlights

- Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering, Iowa State University
- Research experience across academic, industrial, and translational R&D environments
- Lead and co-author publications in journals including *Small*, *Langmuir*, *Chemistry of Materials*, *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces*, *Journal of Colloid and Interface Science*, and *Nanoscale*
- Co-inventor on a U.S. provisional patent related to functional antimicrobial particle and coating technologies
- Experience in semiconductor cleanroom fabrication, thin-film processing, polymer and coating development, and electrochemical materials
- Peer reviewer for journals published by ACS, RSC, Wiley, Springer, and MDPI
- Recipient of research, teaching, and professional recognition, including an MRS Best Poster Award and Iowa State University Graduate College Teaching Excellence Award
