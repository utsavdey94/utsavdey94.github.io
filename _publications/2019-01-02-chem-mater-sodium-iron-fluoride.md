This work introduces a topochemical bottom-up approach for transforming a one-dimensional FeF₃·3H₂O precursor into two-dimensional NaFeF₄ and three-dimensional Na₂Fe₂F₇ frameworks. The resulting Na₂Fe₂F₇ cathode demonstrated reversible capacities above 50 mAh g⁻¹ with >99% Coulombic efficiency and an average intercalation voltage of approximately 3.25 V versus Na⁺/Na⁰.
