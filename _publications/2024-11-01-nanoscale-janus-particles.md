This review provides a comprehensive overview of Janus particle synthesis and applications, with particular emphasis on seeded synthesis approaches for controlling particle size, morphology, composition, and functionality. It discusses applications spanning emulsion stabilization, drug delivery, electronic devices, and coatings, while highlighting scalability and commercialization challenges.
