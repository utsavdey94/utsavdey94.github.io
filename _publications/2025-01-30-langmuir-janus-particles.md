This perspective examines how Janus particles compare with conventional surfactants, dispersants, block copolymers, and related molecular systems. It highlights their distinctive assembly behavior, strong interfacial adsorption, tunable amphiphilicity, and emerging opportunities in sustainable, anti-icing, antifouling, and other functional coatings.
