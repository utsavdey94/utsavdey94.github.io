This work introduces a dilution strategy for controlling Janus particle morphology during seeded emulsion polymerization. By varying dilution and seed cross-linking density, the study establishes a practical morphology map and shows how monomer diffusion governs transitions between spherical, snowman, and dumbbell-like structures.
