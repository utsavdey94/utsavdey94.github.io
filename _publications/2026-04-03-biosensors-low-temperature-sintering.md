This review examines materials and processing strategies for low-temperature sintering in printed bioelectronics, including nanoparticle inks, metal-organic decomposition inks, oxides, chalcogenides, and hybrid systems. It compares thermal, photonic, laser, plasma, microwave, and chemical sintering approaches with respect to conductivity, densification, substrate compatibility, and integration with flexible and biofunctional devices.
