This work develops a polystyrene-core/poly(hydantoin acrylamide)-shell nanoparticle architecture as a scalable antimicrobial additive for waterborne coatings. The core–shell design substantially reduces hydantoin usage while maintaining rechargeable antimicrobial functionality and high antibacterial efficacy against both Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria.
