This work introduces hybrid emulsion-dispersion (HED) polymerization as a one-pot route for synthesizing polymeric Janus nanoparticles without preformed seeds, sequential monomer addition, or post-synthetic modification. The resulting particles exhibit tunable Janus morphologies and preferential interfacial localization in coatings, enabling efficient and rechargeable antibacterial performance.
