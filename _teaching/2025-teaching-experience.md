### Overview
I have **over 4 years of teaching and student mentorship experience** in **chemistry and materials science**, collaborating with faculty to develop lecture notes, demonstration videos, and lab modules that foster **critical thinking, technical competency, and rigorous laboratory practice**.

### 🧪 Graduate & Undergraduate Lab Instruction (MSE 453 / MSE 553)
**Role:** Graduate Teaching Assistant  
**Responsibilities:**
- Mentored graduate and undergraduate students in **polymer synthesis and characterization** — including DLS, zeta potential, and GPC analysis.  
- Trained students in **mechanical testing** (Instron tensile testing), **3D printing** (filament and resin-based systems), and **thermal analysis** (DSC, TGA).  
- Supervised **coating formulation and evaluation**, including rheology, gloss, adhesion, hardness, and contact-angle measurements.  
- Guided students in writing structured lab reports with clear analysis and data interpretation.

### ⚗️ Organic Chemistry Laboratory (CHEM 231L)
**Role:** Teaching Assistant  
**Responsibilities:**

### 🧩 Teaching Philosophy
My teaching approach integrates **hands-on learning**, **conceptual clarity**, and **scientific rigor**. I aim to bridge foundational understanding with modern research applications, preparing students to think critically, design experiments thoughtfully, and communicate results effectively.
